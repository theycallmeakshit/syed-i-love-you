1. Purpose and Scope

Delhi Hazard Watch is an open civic hazard intelligence portal designed to provide situational awareness for urban flooding, underpass waterlogging, and precursor infrastructure risks in the National Capital Territory of Delhi.

2. Telemetry and Community Reports

When citizens submit a waterlogging or infrastructure report:

  • Coordinates: Geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) are recorded solely to locate the reported hazard on the public map.
  • Descriptions: Water depth estimates, road obstruction notes, and photos (if attached) are stored locally in the database.
  • Personal Identifiers: No user registration, phone numbers, email addresses, or personally identifiable information (PII) are required or tracked.

3. Third-Party Map Data and Weather

Cartographic tiles are served via OpenStreetMap and CARTO basemaps. Weather observation telemetry is sourced from the India Meteorological Department (IMD) public bulletins and the Open-Meteo public forecast API. No user data is transmitted to these providers.

4. Local Storage

Preferences such as active visual theme (dark or light) and last inspected hotspot are stored locally in your browser storage. No tracking cookies or advertising pixels are used.

5. Point of Contact

For inquiries regarding telemetry validation or official civic coordination, refer to the Delhi Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) emergency desk or contact the platform maintainers via the civic portal.
