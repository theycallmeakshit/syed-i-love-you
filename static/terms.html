1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or utilizing Delhi Hazard Watch, you acknowledge that this tool is an advisory decision support utility for civic and emergency preparedness across Delhi NCR.

2. Nature of Advisory Data

The platform aggregates multi-source inputs: live weather telemetry from IMD, Central Water Commission flood benchmarks for the Yamuna at Old Railway Bridge, and real-time community waterlogging reports. Risk scores and safe evacuation corridors are computational estimates and should be used alongside instructions from local authorities.

3. Official Emergency Channels

In life-threatening flood or submerged vehicle emergencies, always call official emergency hotlines directly:

  • National Emergency: 112
  • Delhi Disaster Management Authority (DDMA): 1077
  • Central Flood Control Room: 011-22421656

4. Community Submissions

Users submitting hazard reports agree to submit accurate, non-misleading information regarding road conditions and waterlogging. Deliberate submission of falsified flood alerts is strictly prohibited.

5. Limitation of Liability

Delhi Hazard Watch and its contributors shall not be held liable for damages, route delays, or losses resulting from reliance on computational flood simulations or user-submitted status updates.
